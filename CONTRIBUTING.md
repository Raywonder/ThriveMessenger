# Contributing to Thrive Messenger

Thrive Messenger is maintained by Divine Creations as a downstream project. We may
accept outside work when maintainers explicitly open or invite a pull request.
Unsolicited pull requests may be closed without review.

## Before submitting

1. Confirm that a maintainer requested or approved the proposed work.
2. Keep the change focused and preserve accessibility across keyboard and screen
   reader workflows.
3. Identify any upstream project, third-party code, generated content, or assets.
4. State the license and provenance of everything added.
5. Do not include credentials, personal data, private server details, production
   databases, logs, or unredacted operational records.
6. Test the affected platform and document the result.

## Contribution terms

By submitting a pull request, you confirm that you have the right to contribute the
material and that the repository maintainers may use, modify, distribute, and
relicense your contribution as part of Thrive Messenger, subject to rights that
cannot be reassigned or relicensed. Upstream and third-party licenses continue to
govern their respective material.

No contribution is accepted until a maintainer reviews and merges it.
