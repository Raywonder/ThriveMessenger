Thrive Messenger Privacy Choices

These choices apply to accounts on the default TappedIn Thrive Messenger service. For another server, contact that server's operator.

  • Access or correction: ask for a copy or correction of account information associated with your verified account.
  • Deletion: use Settings → Account → Delete Account while signed in to permanently delete the account from the connected server. The app requires confirmation and unlinks locally stored Mastodon, WordPress, and other SSO identities without deleting the external accounts. See the account-deletion instructions.
  • Room content: contact a room owner or server administrator about room messages, membership, or files controlled by that room.
  • Self-hosted service: change the server on the sign-in screen. TappedIn does not control independent servers.

Submit other requests through Thrive Messenger support. We may need to verify account ownership before acting. Thrive Messenger does not offer advertising or cross-app tracking, so there is no advertising opt-out switch.
